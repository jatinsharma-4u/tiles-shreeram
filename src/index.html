01 The brand

A surface is more than what you see. It defines how a space feels.

Hari Traders brings together tiles that are designed to be lived on. We select every surface for its material honesty, the depth of its veining, the grain of its oak, the quiet of its concrete, and for how it performs after years of everyday use.

Explore the collection
  • i.

    Philosophy

    Materials first. A good surface should disappear into a space and make everything around it feel better.

  • ii.

    Design

    Contemporary collections shaped by architecture, calm palettes, honest textures, considered formats.

  • iii.

    Quality

    Rectified edges, consistent shading and dependable strength, checked before anything leaves the warehouse.

  • iv.

    Innovation

    Large slabs, convincing natural looks and finishes engineered for floors, walls and the outdoors.

04 Material & craft

Made with precision.
Designed with purpose.

  1. 01

    Material quality

    Dense, glazed porcelain with consistent shading from the first box to the last.

  2. 02

    Manufacturing

    Rectified edges and tight calibre allow slim grout lines and clean, continuous surfaces.

  3. 03

    Surface finishing

    Polished, satin, matt or textured, each finish tuned to how the room is actually used.

  4. 04

    Durability

    Low water absorption, strong underfoot and resistant to stains, scratches and daily wear.

  5. 05

    Quality control

    Every batch is checked for size, shade and flatness before it is dispatched.
